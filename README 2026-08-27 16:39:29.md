# NorthPeak Retail — Stockout & Markdown Rescue

**The problem, in plain words:** NorthPeak Retail loses money at both ends of the shelf. A **stockout** is when a store has none of a product a shopper came in to buy — the demand was real, but the sale walks out the door (and often the rest of the basket goes to a competitor). A **markdown** is the reverse: product sitting in stores that won't sell at full price, discounted to clear shelf space, giving the margin away. **Both come from the same root cause** — inventory in the wrong store at the wrong time — and nobody sees it until it's too late to move the stock. Across the year that's **~$80M in lost sales to stockouts (~4% of revenue)** and **~$120M in markdowns**. This demo shows NorthPeak catching one live slice of that leak — a cold snap — and fixing it with an AI assistant whose **spend is capped and every recommendation is governed**, so it never just trades one margin leak for another.

| **The live slice** | An early cold snap ~3 weeks ago flipped demand for cold-weather apparel: **sold out in the North** (real demand, lost sales) while the **same styles pile toward markdown in the South** (dead stock, margin clock running) |
| **Investigation** | Priya asks *"Store 214 is short on the Summit Down Parka — what's the best recovery move?"* — the assistant ranks **transfer vs. expedite vs. substitute** by **net recaptured value** (recaptured revenue − cost − margin impact) |
| **Root cause** | Inventory was allocated to a normal-weather plan; the cold snap pulled demand North faster than the batch replenishment cycle could react |
| **The governance angle** | The assistant only ever recommends a move that's **net-positive** — it won't cure a stockout by creating a worse markdown. And its AI spend runs through **Unity AI Gateway**: capped, guard-railed, and logged **per store** so the fix can't become a new open-ended cost |

## Why this lands

Two problems that look opposite are the **same misallocation** seen from two sides — and the demo shows them **on one map, in one glance**: red stockouts in the North sitting next to amber overstock in the South, on the *same five SKUs*. Then it does the hard part: it doesn't just flag the problem, it **ranks the fix by net value** and lets Priya act in one click — with the AI that assists **governed and spend-bounded end to end**. Governed data → a governed recommendation → a governed, bounded AI assistant.

## The demo arc (what the finished solution shows)

1. **See it** — open the Store Ops app: a US map, red stockouts in the North next to amber overstock in the South on the same SKUs, with lost-sales + markdown KPIs.
2. **Ask why** — in the chat dock, ask why Store 214 is short; the assistant investigates via Genie over the governed lakehouse.
3. **Get the move** — the assistant ranks transfer / expedite / substitute by **net recaptured value** and recommends the transfer, with a what-if. It will *not* recommend a move that trades the stockout for a worse markdown or a margin loss.
4. **Act** — approve → the move + a markdown-hold write back to Lakebase → the queue and KPIs update live.
5. **Governed & bounded AI** — every assistant call runs through Unity AI Gateway (spend cap, guardrails, per-store attributable logging) — the fix can't become a new open-ended cost.
